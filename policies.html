policies

Deposits

A 5 OMR deposit confirms your appointment. It goes toward your service and can’t be refunded under any circumstances.

Rescheduling

You can reschedule up to 24 hours before your appointment.

Cancellations

Cancelling means losing your deposit.

No guests

No extra guests or children during appointments, so the studio stays calm and relaxing for everyone.

Be on time

Other clients may be booked after you. There’s a 30-minute grace period. After that your appointment is cancelled or rescheduled and the deposit is forfeited.
